LÝ THUYẾT: THÌ QUÁ KHỨ TIẾP DIỄN

1. Cấu trúc

1.1. Khẳng định: S + was/were + V-ing

• She was cooking dinner at 5 p.m yesterday.
• They were playing badminton when I came yesterday.

1.2. Phủ định: S + wasn’t/ weren’t + V-ing

• He wasn’t working when his boss came yesterday.
• We weren’t watching TV at 9 p.m yesterday.

1.3. Câu hỏi: Was/ Were + S + V-ing?

• Was your mother going to the market at 7 a.m yesterday?
• Were they staying with you when I called you yesterday?

2. Cách sử dụng

2.1. Hành động đang xảy ra tại thời điểm xác định trong quá khứ:

At 12 o’clock yesterday, we were having lunch.

2.2. Hành động đang xảy ra (QKTD) thì hành động khác xen vào (QKĐ):

Dùng "When" hoặc "As":

• I was watching TV when he phoned me.
• As we were playing football, the bell rang.

Mẹo: Hành động dài -> QKTD (was/were Ving). Hành động ngắn -> QKĐ (Ved/V2).

2.3. Hai hành động xảy ra song song:

Dùng "While":

• While Ellen was reading, Tim was watching television.

3. Dấu hiệu nhận biết

  • at + giờ + thời gian quá khứ (at 8 p.m last night)
  • at this time + thời gian quá khứ (at this time yesterday)
  • all day yesterday, all yesterday morning
  • Khi có When, While, As trong câu kể về quá khứ.
