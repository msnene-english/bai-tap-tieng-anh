📚 LÝ THUYẾT — THÌ QUÁ KHỨ ĐƠN VỚI TO BE

Dùng was / were khi nói về điều đã xảy ra và kết thúc trong quá khứ: He was tired. · They were in the room.
🟢 1. Chọn was hay were?
Chủ ngữKhẳng định (+)Phủ định (−)
I · He · She · It
danh từ SỐ ÍT		waswas not = wasn’t
You · We · They
danh từ SỐ NHIỀU		werewere not = weren’t
He was tired. · They were in the room. · He wasn’t at school yesterday. · They weren’t in the park.
🔵 2. Câu nghi vấn & trả lời ngắn
Đưa Was / Were lên đầu câu: Was + I / he / she / it + …?  ·  Were + you / we / they + …?
Were they tired yesterday? ➜ Yes, they were. / No, they weren’t.
Was he at home? ➜ Yes, he was. / No, he wasn’t.
⚠️ Câu hỏi với you thì trả lời bằng I: Were you at home yesterday? ➜ Yes, I was.
🟠 3. There was / There were
Nhìn danh từ đứng sau: số ít ➜ There was · số nhiều ➜ There were.
There was a cinema near here. · There were two beds in the room.
🟣 4. Dấu hiệu nhận biết quá khứ đơn
yesterday · last night / week / month / year / Sunday · … ago (two days ago) · in + năm (in 2010) · this morning · when I was a kid…
🔑 5. Ba mẹo tránh sai
① Có and nối hai người ➜ chủ ngữ SỐ NHIỀU: Mandy and Sue were…
② Câu đã có to be thì KHÔNG dùng didn’t: ✘ He didn’t tired ➜ ✔ He wasn’t tired.
③ Một câu có thể có hai chủ ngữ khác nhau — xét riêng từng chủ ngữ: The students were noisy so the teacher was angry.
