📘 REPORTED SPEECH — 4 dạng câu tường thuật

🗣️ Câu kể: said (that) / told + sb (that) + S + V lùi thì — “I am tired.” → She said (that) she was tired.
📣 Mệnh lệnh: told / asked + sb + (not) to V — “Don’t run!” → He told us not to run.
❓ Câu hỏi Yes/No: asked / wondered / wanted to know + if / whether + S + V · Wh-: asked + wh- + S + V (không đảo, bỏ dấu ?)
⏪ Lùi thì: am/is → was · do → did · did / have done → had done · will → would · can → could  |  Đổi: now → then · today → that day · tomorrow → the next day · yesterday → the day before · here → there · this → that
